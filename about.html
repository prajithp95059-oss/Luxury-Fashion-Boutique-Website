Our Mission

Designing garments that age like heirlooms, not trends.

Stackly began in a single-room workshop in Chennai in 2008, with three tailors and a shared conviction: that clothing should be made to last generations, not seasons. Today, that conviction still drives every collection — from the mills we source from, to the hands that finish each seam.

We work in small batches by design. Every silhouette passes through a founder-led design review, a hand-draping stage, and a private fitting before it ever reaches a client — a process that resists shortcuts, and always will.

Explore Our Services

From sketch to seam, nothing is rushed.

Every piece moves through four deliberate stages before it earns the Stackly label.

01

Sketch & Design

Every silhouette starts as a hand-drawn sketch, reviewed and refined by our founder before it's cut.

02

Fabric Selection

We source directly from mills in Mysore, Varanasi, and Lyon, hand-checking every bolt for weight and drape.

03

Hand Draping

Cloth is draped directly on a form, letting the fabric's own weight guide the final pattern.

04

Finishing & Fitting

A private fitting closes the process, with hand-finished seams adjusted to the client's exact form.
