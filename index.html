Materials

Sourced from mills that still weave by hand.

Every Stackly piece begins with a fabric, not a sketch. We travel to the mills ourselves — silk from Kanchipuram's family looms, linen from a century-old Belgian weaver, pashmina hand-spun in Kashmir — and buy only what a season truly needs.

Meet Our Weavers
Silk Dupioni

Woven on family looms in Kanchipuram — prized for its natural sheen and structured drape.

Belgian Linen

From a century-old Belgian weaver — breathable, textured, and built to soften with age.

Pashmina Wool

Hand-spun in Kashmir from the finest wool blend — warm without ever feeling heavy.

Chanderi Cotton

Hand-loomed in Madhya Pradesh — sheer, lightweight, and finished with a soft golden sheen.

Signature Piece

The Ribbon Clutch, hand-stitched in silk dupioni.

Named for the satin ribbon that traces every Stackly runway, this season's signature clutch is finished entirely by hand — twelve hours of stitching, one hidden monogram, and a clasp cast in-house from reclaimed brass.

Our Commitment

Beautiful clothing shouldn't cost the earth, or the maker.

01

Zero-Waste Cutting

Offcuts are repurposed into linings and accessories — nothing leaves the atelier as waste.

02

Fair Wage Ateliers

Every artisan is salaried, not piece-rated, with healthcare included from day one.

03

Heritage Partnerships

We work directly with family-run mills, cutting out exploitative middle layers entirely.
